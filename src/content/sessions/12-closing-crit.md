## Before the lab

Bring your final project in whatever state it's actually in, and the
co-writing log from week 10. There is no expectation of a finished
project this week — the final project isn't due until after the lab is
over.

## In the lab

Five-minute slots, one per student: what you're building, what's working,
what you're worried about. No slide deck required — a working build or a
draft, shown directly, is preferred over a summary of one.

The last question in every slot is the same, deliberately, following on
from this week's lecture: does your own project's design make a suggestion
anywhere, and if so, whose interest does that suggestion actually serve?
Most people haven't thought about their own work this way until asked
directly, which is the point of asking in the room rather than leaving it
for the write-up.

## Afterwards

Feedback from this session is meant to change what you submit, not just
how you feel about it. If a specific piece of feedback changes your final
project, that's worth a line in your submission saying so.
