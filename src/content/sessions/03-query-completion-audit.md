## Before the lab

No setup beyond a browser and a willingness to type slowly. You'll be
working with whatever search box you use most, so there's nothing to
install.

## In the lab

Everyone runs the same short list of neutral prefixes — starts of names, of
places, of common questions — through their chosen search box, and records
every suggestion offered. Comparing lists across the room does most of the
work: the same prefix on the same search engine, typed by two people in the
same lab, sometimes returns different suggestions because of personalisation
you can't see directly but can infer from the difference.

Then a narrower pass: prefixes chosen specifically to probe for the kind of
suggestion a platform would rather not show — and a look at what's absent
rather than what's present, since the interesting evidence here is usually a
gap.

## Afterwards

This lab's method — a fixed prefix list, run and compared across several
systems — is close to the shape Assignment 2's audit wants from you later in
the semester, just aimed at a different kind of suggestion. Keep your
prefix list; you'll reuse the method before you reuse the topic.
