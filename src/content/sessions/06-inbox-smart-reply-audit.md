## Before the lab

Bring a device with an email or messaging app that offers suggested
replies, and the ability to screenshot or otherwise record what it
suggests. You are auditing your own inbox, not a shared example set, because
the suggestions are personalised to you specifically.

## In the lab

Scroll back through recent messages, screenshotting the suggested-reply row
for each one before you act on it. Ten is the minimum; the more varied the
messages, the more useful the audit — mix routine logistics with anything
emotionally loaded that came through the same channel.

Group discussion focuses on the near-misses rather than the outright wrong
ones, since those are more revealing: a suggestion that's almost right, but
slightly too cheerful or slightly too brief for what it's replying to,
shows the model's actual granularity better than a clean failure does.

## Afterwards

If nothing in your ten messages was wrong, that's a real result, not a
failed lab — say so and say why you think your inbox didn't trigger a
failure, which is itself evidence about how these systems are scoped.
