Freya researches persuasive and manipulative interface design, with a
particular interest in what happens when persuasion is delivered as a
suggestion rather than a demand — a pre-filled reply, a recommended amount,
a completed sentence that happens to serve the platform more than the
person finishing it. She has spent the last few years cataloguing what she
calls "consent by autocomplete": design patterns where the easiest path
through an interface is also the one someone else picked for you.

She joins for a single lecture, in the course's final week, to connect
everything the semester built — completion in text, in code, in images, in
forecasts — back to the question of who a suggestion is actually working
for.
